HOW TO EDIT PORTFOLIO CONTENT

The two editable files now sit directly in the ROOT of the repository:

films.txt
art.txt

They should be visible next to:
index.html
showreels.html

FILMS
-----
Each line:
filename | title | description | link | crop position

Example:
film-10.webp | NEW PROJECT | Direction / Short film | projects/new-project.html | center

ART
---
Each line:
filename | title | medium / size

Example:
art-10.webp | THE DANCER | Oil on canvas, 70 x 100 cm

IMPORTANT:
- assets/ still contains all image files
- films.txt and art.txt must sit next to index.html
- do not place them in another folder
